# Guillaume Beaudouin

Master's student in economic and financial engineering at Université de Rennes, working on energy and carbon markets, market regimes and systematic trading research.

**Energy and carbon**
- [electricite-france](https://github.com/Guillaume-Beaudouin-Git/electricite-france): how French electricity demand follows temperature (2013–2024), and what a battery would have earned on the day-ahead market (2015–2026). In French, with an English summary.
- [encheres-carbone](https://github.com/Guillaume-Beaudouin-Git/encheres-carbone): EU carbon allowance auctions on EEX, 2013–2026: supply, demand, and a test that finds no evidence that the cover ratio predicts the next clearing prices.

**Macro and regimes**
- [regime-lab](https://github.com/Guillaume-Beaudouin-Git/regime-lab): a Master's team project of two, on a machine-learning model of market regimes that recognises US recessions and predicts volatility, not returns.

**Systematic research**
- [proof-of-edge](https://github.com/Guillaume-Beaudouin-Git/proof-of-edge): nine trading studies written up in full, with verdicts (walk-forward tests, multiple-testing control), and the self-reported record of an automated FTMO account (simulated capital, real payouts).
  - full working history: [research-archive](https://github.com/Guillaume-Beaudouin-Git/research-archive)
